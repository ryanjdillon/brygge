# Accounting decisions

See [README.md](README.md) for the format. Implementation: [`backend/internal/accounting/README.md`](../../backend/internal/accounting/README.md); rules: [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md).

### ACC-1: Keep the books as if MVA-registered

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-29 · BRY-446
- **Decision:** Brygge's bookkeeping follows the rules for an MVA-registered, bookkeeping-obliged organisation, whether or not a given club is registered today. In particular, any change to the amount of a sale (a refund, a price reduction) is documented by a credit note against the original faktura or order, never by a bare ledger entry.
- **Why:** a club may cross the MVA registration threshold later, and other organisations deploying Brygge may already be registered. Bookkeeping that is compliant for the strictest case is still correct for a club that isn't obliged, while the reverse isn't true. The rule for credit notes is Regnskapsstiftelsen's GBS 1 (*Kreditnota – innhold, utstedelse, bokføring og oppbevaring*).
- **Consequences:** automatic workflows generate the documents a compliant club needs (see ACC-2), so compliance costs the treasurer no extra work. Faktura lines don't carry MVA rates or accounts yet; that is separate, filed work, and credit notes copy lines as they are until it lands.

### ACC-2: A refund that reduces a sale issues a credit note automatically

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-29 · BRY-446
- **Decision:** when a card refund on a faktura or an order is booked, Brygge issues a credit note for the refunded amount (lines copied from the faktura or order, scaled proportionally for a partial refund), books it (DR revenue / CR 1500), links it to the faktura, and sends it to the payer. The refund itself books DR 1500 / CR the provider clearing account. A partial refund leaves the faktura paid; the refund-generated credit note never counts as credit owed to the member, because the refund already paid it.
- **Why:** of the options considered, booking the refund straight against revenue without a credit note is not compliant (ACC-1); a manual credit note per refund creates work and is easy to forget; sending every partial refund to manual review creates work for no added safety. Automatic generation is compliant and costs nothing per refund.
- **Consequences:** refund credit notes are linked to the faktura through their refund, not through the manual credit link, so the rule that a manual credit equals the faktura's total (BRY-342) is unchanged. A faktura whose refund credit notes add up to its total counts as credited. A charge that the treasurer assigned to an account by hand isn't a sale through Brygge, so its refund books against that account with no credit note.

### ACC-3: A full refund of a faktura waits for the treasurer's choice

- **Status:** superseded by ACC-8 · 2026-10-29 · BRY-446
- **Decision:** a full refund means either the sale is cancelled (**credit the faktura**) or the money must be collected another way (**collect again**). A refund started in Brygge asks which, with crediting as the default. A full refund made in the provider's dashboard holds the faktura as "refunded – awaiting a choice": no reminders, no pay link, and a task for the treasurer with the same two one-click choices.
- **Why:** only the treasurer knows why the money went back. Reopening the faktura automatically would send reminders to a member who was just refunded; crediting automatically would write off money the club may still be owed.
- **Consequences:** "collect again" reopens the receivable and detaches the faktura from the refunded order so that a new payment link can be made.

### ACC-4: A disputed card payment is a loss until the club wins

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-29 · BRY-446
- **Decision:** when the card provider withdraws disputed money, Brygge books it to a per-club dispute account (default 7830 Tap på fordringer) plus the dispute fee to the fee account, and reverses it if the dispute is won. The faktura stays paid. The treasurer is emailed and gets a task.
- **Why:** simpler than holding the amount on a "disputed" receivable and moving it at the outcome, and a dispute is rare. The member did pay; whether to invoice again after a lost dispute is a board decision, not an automatic one.

### ACC-5: A provider payment is booked only once the provider confirms it

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-27 · BRY-440, BRY-445
- **Decision:** the ledger and the faktura's paid state follow confirmed provider events (captured charges, refund balance transactions), booked by the provider booking pass. A click in Brygge (a refund request) or a webhook never books optimistically.
- **Why:** a request can fail or be reversed after the click; the provider's balance transaction is the only record the payout reconciles against.
- **Consequences:** webhooks for refunds and disputes keep only the provider state tables up to date; order and faktura state change in the booking pass.

### ACC-6: Fakturas imported from a previous system are never booked without an opening balance

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-26 · BRY-386, BRY-365
- **Decision:** importing fakturas creates records only, with no journal entries. A payment or credit can be booked against an imported faktura only once an opening-balance entry carries it. Which imported fakturas were unpaid at cutover comes from the previous system's open-items list, not from bank matching.
- **Why:** booking DR bank / CR 1500 for an imported faktura with no opening balance drives 1500 negative.
- **Consequences:** the credit link and the bank assignment refuse imported fakturas without a qualifying opening balance. See [migrating-from-previous-system.md](../user/migrating-from-previous-system.md).

### ACC-7: Exemptions are configured per club, with no defaults

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-16 · BRY-351
- **Decision:** exemptions (for example a free slip for a board role) are configured as role × price item per club, and the configuration starts empty. An exempt faktura is still issued and booked gross, with an exemption line, and nets to 0 kr.
- **Why:** which roles get a perk differs between clubs (see PLAT-1); booking gross keeps the cost of the perk visible in the accounts.

### ACC-8: A full refund credits the faktura automatically

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-29 · BRY-446 · supersedes ACC-3
- **Decision:** a full card refund credits the faktura automatically (a credit note per ACC-2), whether it was made in Brygge or in the provider's dashboard. A refund started in Brygge can choose **Krev inn på nytt** instead, if no partial refund was credited before: no credit note, and the faktura reopens as an ordinary unpaid faktura. After an automatic credit, the treasurer collects again by issuing a new faktura.
- **Why:** holding a dashboard refund until the treasurer chooses (ACC-3) leaves the ledger and the faktura list disagreeing for as long as nobody decides, possibly across year-end, and needs a waiting state, reminder suppression and a task. Crediting is the usual reason for a full refund, so making it the automatic outcome is the least work and keeps the books consistent.

### ACC-9: An issued credit note is never voided

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-29 · BRY-446
- **Decision:** a credit note made by a refund is an issued sales document and stays. If the refund later fails and the money comes back, or the refund booking is corrected, Brygge issues a new faktura for the amount instead of voiding the credit note.
- **Why:** under ACC-1 a sales document that has been issued is corrected by a new document, not withdrawn.

### ACC-10: Vipps income parked on 3900 is reclassified through the price-item rules

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-30 · BRY-557
- **Decision:** the Jan-Jun 2026 Vipps charges that the legacy model parked on 3900 Andre inntekter and that were then posted are reclassified through the club's price-item rules, the same rules automatic Vipps booking uses, by an explicit treasurer action.
- **Why:** automatic booking now classifies new charges, so the parked ones are the only remainder, and the price-item rules already say which amount is which item.
- **Consequences:** `ApplyVippsFallbackReclass`; see [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "A posted charge on 3900 may be reclassified" and [the accounting README](../../backend/internal/accounting/README.md). These follow from how it was built, not from a separate owner decision:
  - the entry is reversed and rebooked (`rebookVippsChargeTx`) rather than edited, so the charge's own entry stays its booking and the payout clearing is untouched; Angre treats the replacement like any charge booking (in a cleared settlement only after the deposit is undone, ACC-11);
  - a charge the rules cannot place unambiguously stays on 3900 with the reason shown;
  - a charge matching a member's open faktura is left to Tildel, because assigning it settles a faktura and a reclass only moves revenue;
  - a charge that may already have paid a faktura (`receivableChargeSettledFaktura`) stays, so a double count is not hidden behind a revenue account;
  - only open periods are touched; reopening a closed year is a separate, deliberate step.

### ACC-11: A charge in a cleared Vipps settlement is not unbooked on its own

- **Status:** proposed pending owner confirmation · 2026-10-30 · BRY-555
- **Decision:** while a Vipps settlement has a posted clearing (its fee and payout booked against the clearing account), a posted charge in it cannot be unassigned or voided on its own. The refusal offers **Angre innbetalingen**, which undoes the settlement's payout clearing (the payout bilag is voided and the deposit returns to Tildel; the fee stays booked); the charge is then corrected and the next sync clears the settlement again. Changing only what a charge books (its revenue account or its faktura), with the same clearing debit, is done atomically and does not undo the clearing. A charge counts as booked for a clearing only when its entry is posted, and the draft backlog posts a settlement's fee and payout only when all its charges are posted.
- **Why:** an unbook after the clearing leaves the fee and payout credits on 2900 with no charge debit against them until someone books the charge again, and nothing in the product says it must be.
- **Alternatives:** (1) allow the unbook and rely on the treasurer to rebook: 2900 is wrong in between and the books can be left that way; (2) undo the clearing automatically inside the unbook: it hides a bank-ledger action behind a charge button and returns a deposit to Tildel without the treasurer deciding; (3) offer only the atomic rebook and refuse every isolated unbook outright: a refunded or misbooked charge could then never be returned to Tildel, because nothing would undo the clearing it sits in; the chosen design adds that undo.
- **Consequences:** correcting a charge in a cleared settlement takes one extra confirmed step, the clearing undo, unless only the account or faktura changes. `rebookVippsChargeTx` is the one place that replaces a posted charge's booking in a cleared settlement.

### ACC-12: A year whose books stay in the previous system is a reference year in Brygge

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-10-30 · BRY-418
- **Decision:** a fiscal period can be marked *reference only*. Its data stays: nothing is voided or deleted. But it isn't the club's books in Brygge. It is left out of every queue, count, badge, alert and automatic booking, and out of every balance carried into a later year; the opening balance carries those (ACC-6). Its reports still open when the year is chosen, marked as a reference year, and momskompensasjon reads it as usual. The only bookings it takes are costs: an entry may touch only cost accounts and bank accounts, with at least one cost line (an outgoing bank row booked to a cost account, a cost moved between cost accounts, a supplier refund into the bank), so that the year's momskompensasjon can still be filed from Brygge. Closing the year after filing stops those too. Its drafts that are not costs stay drafts and are never posted. The year's incoming bank rows appear nowhere, and its outgoing rows are listed only under Tildel's **Referanseår** filter, where they can be booked to a cost account or dismissed.
- **Why:** the year was imported for comparison. Booking its remaining deposits, Vipps drafts and transfers would duplicate what the previous system's annual accounts already hold, and working through them is more trouble than it's worth. Refusing every posting (the first version of this decision) would have blocked filing momskompensasjon for the year. A flag rather than a fourth period status, because the year must stay open for cost bookings and later be closed while remaining a reference year, and about 15 places test `status != 'open'`.
- **Consequences:** one guard in journal create, post and void refuses any other entry in a reference period, or dated in one, with 409 `reference_period`. Queues and automatic passes filter the year's rows by their own booking date. The cumulative balance sheet skips reference periods other than the one selected. The current year and a year holding an opening balance can't be marked. A provider settlement still in flight at year-end (charges booked in the reference year, deposit paid in the next) is carried in the opening balance as a debit on its clearing account, dated 1 January. Its charges and fee stay as reference-year entries that never post, so the new-year deposit is booked against that debit: it already is, when a posted entry cleared it; otherwise a manual bilag (debit bank, credit clearing) books it and its bank row is dismissed, because the sweep either refuses its fee entry or never finds its charges posted (ACC-11). See [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "A reference period books only costs and carries nothing forward" and [migrating-from-previous-system.md](../user/migrating-from-previous-system.md).

### ACC-13: Brygge books and clears unambiguous provider payments automatically, as a system actor

- **Status:** proposed pending owner confirmation · 2026-11-01 · BRY-554, BRY-455. Confirmed when the owner turns the switch on in production.
- **Decision:** when a club turns on **Bokfør og avstem automatisk** (off by default), the provider jobs do more than fetch. The booking runs right after every provider import (each Vipps Report API pull, and Stripe as soon as Stripe reports an event), at every start of the API (which includes every deploy), and as a nightly safety net; it is never night-only. It runs the same booking passes a person's **Synk Vipps** or **Synkroniser** runs: order-linked Vipps charges, the opted-in automatic Vipps and bank rules, the Vipps payout sweep, and the Stripe booking pass and payout sweep. It books as the club's system actor, a non-person user named "Automatisk bokføring" that cannot log in, holds no role and appears in no member list. Its bilags are marked **Automatisk**. Each run that changed, failed or was skipped writes one summary audit row. The ambiguity rules, the club's existing switches and every guard (reference years, closed periods, the posted-only clearing gate, faktura locks, a settlement that does not net to 0) apply unchanged. Proposed pending owner confirmation:
  - (a) a card refund's credit note is issued as soon as the refund is booked, and is emailed to the payer at once, as ACC-8 says. A refund made from Brygge already issues its credit note at once. A refund made in Stripe's dashboard is booked when Stripe reports it, so its credit note is issued and sent from Brygge just as promptly (BRY-570; today the Stripe webhook only ingests a refund and booking still needs a person's Synkroniser run, and the **Automatisk** badge and the operator alerts below are later steps of BRY-554 too); the nightly run is the safety net for one that was missed, never the only time it is booked;
  - (b) a person's undo of a booking or clearing is final for the automatic passes: the row is left out of every automatic rule until a person books it (a person booking a payment in a settlement lifts it from that settlement's payout deposit, so ACC-11's "the next sync clears the settlement again" holds);
  - (c) no automatic Vipps pass ever opens a fiscal year, whoever starts it, as bank auto-assign and Stripe already do. A payment dated in a year the treasurer has not opened waits, listed with the reason "no fiscal year for <year>". The first pass after that year exists books it in its own year, with the entry dated the payment's own date; it is never booked into another year. The waiting payment also becomes a task in the treasurer's BRD-1 task inbox, with a notification (BRY-575).
- **Why:** the Jul-Oct 2026 Vipps backlog came from booking that ran only when someone clicked, while the nightly job fetched the payments and left them. BRD-1 asks for safe steps to run by themselves. A system user keeps every "who did it" column a real reference, and keeps "no acting user" a refusal, so a request that lost its user can never book as automatic. A sticky undo keeps a person's correction from being reversed by the next run.
- **Alternatives:** nullable `created_by` with an actor-kind flag: turns every missing-user bug into a silent automatic booking and loosens constraints on five tables. Reusing the existing switches as the trigger: order-linked booking and the payout sweep have no switch, so every deploying club would start booking unasked (PLAT-1). A dry-run mode first: the passes are the ones the treasurer already runs by hand, every booking can be undone, and the undo now sticks.
- **Consequences:** under ACC-8, a card refund's credit note is issued and emailed when the refund is booked, which is when the provider reports it or at the latest in the nightly run, not after the next time someone clicks sync. After **Angre innbetalingen** (ACC-11) the deposit stays out of the sweep until a payment in the settlement is booked again, or a person clears it with **Avstem Vipps-utbetaling**. A manual **Synk Vipps** on 1 January no longer opens the year either. Stale-sync, unbooked-charge and uncleared-payout alerts (VippsReportSyncStale, VippsChargesUnbooked, VippsPayoutNotCleared) reach the operator when the run stops working. It is built in steps: the system actor, `users.kind`, the club switch and the `auto_declined_at` columns come first (migration 000129); the passes that use them follow. No code path changes `users.kind` after the row is created. See [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "The system actor is not a person" and the [accounting README](../../backend/internal/accounting/README.md) "System actor". Added 2026-11-11 (BRY-578): a bank upload and **Synkroniser** do not open the year either. This applies the rule BRY-562 put in invariants.md ("No automatic pass creates a fiscal period": an automatic pass never opens a year, whoever triggers it) to the bank passes, and does not widen decision (c): the KID cascade, intra-bank transfer pairing and the legacy Vipps payout pass leave a payment in a year without a period unbooked and list the year as "Ingen regnskapsår for <år>". Added 2026-11-11 (BRY-565), how decision (b) was built, not a new owner decision: the decline is lifted by any person's booking of the row, not only by a booking in Tildel's queue (a person's link of a row always clears it), and from a settlement's payout deposit by a person booking any charge of that settlement (Søk faktura, Lag bilag, the backlog apply, the 3900 reclass), in a short transaction of its own after that booking. Every automatic rule honours it, also those decision (b)'s list leaves implicit: intra-bank transfer pairing, the legacy Vipps payout pass, mapping rules and the Stripe payout sweep; a person's manual clearing (Tildel's **Utlign utbetaling** for Vipps, the action this record calls **Avstem Vipps-utbetaling** above; **Avstem mot Stripe-utbetaling** for Stripe) still books a declined deposit. Dismissing a charge (a refund) lifts nothing, so after **Angre innbetalingen** and a dismissed refund the deposit waits for that manual clearing. Bank reimports keep a declined row instead of replacing it from the file. See [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "A person's undo is final for the automatic passes".
  - **(c)'s notification is built as an email (BRY-575, 2026-11-11, owner decisions).** The BRD-1 task inbox does not exist yet, so the waiting payment is told by email now and becomes an inbox card later (575b, blocked by BRY-485/486; routing by function by BRY-490/482). The email goes to `clubs.treasurer_email`, as a card dispute's does (ACC-4), else to the board notification address; with neither it waits, failed with a note, and is sent once an address is set. Its button opens **Bilag** with the action prefilled (one click and one confirm), never a direct create. Years that are closed or locked are told too, not only missing ones, with their own wording: reopening a closed year needs a fresh TOTP, asked for when the link is opened (the AGENTS.md click-time rule), and the email asks the treasurer to close the year again once the sync has booked the payments. A **locked** year cannot be reopened in the app (`ReopenPeriod` reopens only `closed`, and manual Tildel refuses closed and locked periods alike), so its email says the year must be unlocked outside the app and its link only opens the period; no email offers booking by hand. The condition is derived from the queues, never stored: one notice per club, year and state (and per closing of a closed period), dropped unsent when the condition has cleared by the time it is mailed. See [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "A payment waiting for a fiscal year notifies the treasurer once, derived, never stored".
  - **The automatic run includes the exact bank passes (owner decision, 2026-11-11, BRY-567).** The run books bank rows matched exactly by KID, arkivref or faktura number: the KID cascade without its counterpart-name + amount step. It also pairs transfers between the club's own bank accounts, each pair as a **draft** bilag that a person posts. Name + amount guesses stay excluded: they are what bank auto-assign replaces. The KID step books a payment whatever its amount, the same as an upload does. Why: uploads already run these passes, so the run only catches rows that failed at upload (the year was not open yet, an opening balance came later, a lock was taken). This applies (c)'s "the first pass after that year exists books it" without waiting for a person to click **Synkroniser**.
  - **How the run was built (BRY-567, 2026-11-11), not a new owner decision.** The run covers Vipps and bank; Stripe follows in BRY-570, which reuses the run's trigger constants, summary audit action and observer hook. It also runs the payment back-link heals (no postings) and, when the club opted in, bank auto-assign. It leaves the stranded-draft heal, the provisional-row supersede and the legacy Vipps confirm to a person's **Synkroniser**, so a legacy-model club is never booked unattended. The operator Report API sync now books only on the per-charge model, as the CSV upload and **Synk Vipps** already did. Transfer pairing, by **Synkroniser** and by the run, never pairs a row a person dismissed (the "Intern overføring" workaround for a transfer booked by hand) or paired as a refund. See [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "The automatic booking run books only what a person's sync would, as the system actor" and the [accounting README](../../backend/internal/accounting/README.md) "Automatic booking run".
  - **Operator alerts for unbooked charges and uncleared payouts count only clubs with automatic booking on (owner decision, 2026-11-11, BRY-554; built in BRY-571).** The VippsChargesUnbooked (7 days) and VippsPayoutNotCleared alerts look only at clubs that have **Bokfør og avstem automatisk** on. Why: for a club that books by hand, a Vipps payment or payout waiting in Tildel is the normal state, not a failure of the run, so paging the operator about it would be noise.
  - **Added (BRY-570), 2026-11-12: correction to (a), and how the Stripe run was built.** (a)'s "A refund made from Brygge already issues its credit note at once" was not true when written: the refund button and the refund webhook only kept `payment_refunds`, and every card refund, made in Brygge or in Stripe's dashboard, was booked and had its credit note issued only at the next person's **Synk Stripe**, **Synkroniser** or bank upload. Since BRY-570 both are booked as soon as Stripe reports them, which is what (a) decided: with **Bokfør og avstem automatisk** on, the Stripe webhook kicks an automatic Stripe run after it handled a capture (`checkout.session.completed`, `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`, `payment_intent.succeeded`), a refund (`charge.refunded`, `charge.refund.updated`), a dispute's funds moving (`charge.dispute.funds_withdrawn`, `charge.dispute.funds_reinstated`) or a payout (`payout.paid`, `payout.failed`, `payout.canceled`, after the payout's ingest). The run waits 30 s for more events (at most 2 minutes after the first), then ingests the balance and books and sweeps as the system actor. The webhook still books nothing and booking still reads only the balance (ACC-5). The run also starts at every start of the API and nightly, the safety net for a missed event; with the switch off a kicked run does nothing and the nightly run only ingests, as before. Not a new owner decision: it builds (a) and the record's "Stripe as soon as Stripe reports an event". Owner verification left open: that Stripe's balance listing shows a refund's balance transaction within the 30 s debounce in live mode (else the nightly run books it). See [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "The automatic Stripe run books as the system actor; a webhook only kicks it".
  - **Added 2026-11-12 (BRY-672), how decision (b) was built, not a new owner decision.** After a person undoes an automatic transfer pair, both rows are declined and no automatic pass pairs them again; a person re-pairs them in Tildel with **Par som overføring** (`PairBankTransfer`). It is a person's booking like the other Tildel assigns: the bilag is posted at once (only automatic pairing leaves a draft for a person to post), it lifts the decline on both rows, and it may open the year, as the manual Tildel assigns and the group booking do. The rows must be on two of the club's bank accounts, opposite and equal, at most 14 days apart and in one fiscal period, which must be open; the bilag is dated on the later date (the group booking's and PairRefund's precedent, BRY-380's 14-day window). An incoming row carrying the KID of one of the club's fakturas is refused: it is a faktura payment. A dismissed row cannot be paired. **Angre** undoes it and declines both rows again. The old manual-transfer workaround (a **Nytt bilag** plus dismissing both rows as "Intern overføring") is no longer needed; bilags booked that way stay as they are. The unused `POST /bank-import/{importID}/rows/{rowID}/match` route was removed: it booked one row with arbitrary accounts outside the money gate, so a transfer needed two calls (a double booking). See [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "Transfer pairing never pairs a dismissed or refund-paired row, and keeps no orphan draft".

### ACC-14: Unpaid self-service join fakturas are credited, never deleted, after 60 days

- **Status:** accepted · 2026-11-06 · BRY-476, BRY-631
- **Decision:** a self-service join left unpaid for 60 days after its last activity (the join order's last change, which a re-confirm moves, or the join faktura's issue) ends automatically:
  - **Never billed** (only the pending join order): the order is expired (marked `failed`, its pay token cleared) and the applicant is deactivated. No `users` row is deleted. With no faktura, `PurgeExpiredMemberPII` erases their identity on its next run.
  - **Billed** (the applicant chose **Betal med bank**): the join faktura is credited in full by a credit note ("Innmelding ikkje fullført") linked as a full credit and emailed, and the applicant is deactivated, so the purge erases them after the retention period.
  - **Part of the money in** (a bank row with the faktura's KID or assigned to it, or a reserved or captured card payment): neither credited nor deactivated; the board is told once to review it.
- **The join faktura and its credit note book as the club's system actor.** **Betal med bank** issues the faktura (sets `sent_at`) as it is created, because the KID shown to the applicant is the issue, and books it as the system actor; the credit note is issued the same way and booked when its faktura is. When the system actor cannot be resolved, nothing books as the applicant: `SyncInvoices` books every sent faktura, so the pair always ends up in the GL together. This relies on ACC-13's system actor, which is proposed pending owner confirmation.
- **Why:** an issued faktura is a sales document; under ACC-1 it is corrected by a credit note, never deleted or voided, and faktura numbers come from MAX+1 per club, so a deleted row could also give its number away again. Deactivating rather than deleting a never-billed applicant keeps one erasure path (the purge) and leaves the audit log untouched. A partial payment needs a person to decide between refunding and collecting the rest.
- **Consequences:** no invoice row is ever deleted by the expiry, and numbering runs on. **Betal med bank** turns the join order into the faktura's pay order (its line dropped, its purpose moved to the faktura, an `order_sources` link added), so the faktura is the one payable thing whichever way the applicant pays; it is refused while an online payment of the order is in progress. A payment that arrives after the credit is not matched automatically, because the faktura is settled for the member; it waits for the treasurer. A capture that lands on an expired join order is withheld and told to the board. See [invariants.md](../developer/reference/invariants.md) "An unpaid join expires after 60 days" and `handlers/join_expiry.go`.
