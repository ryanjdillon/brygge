# Leaving the club (Meld deg ut)

> Audience: members who want to leave the club, and the board members who follow up.

## For members: cancelling your membership

You cancel your membership yourself in the member portal, under **Min profil** → **Meld deg ut**.

What happens when you cancel:

- **You stay a member until the end of the year.** The cancellation takes effect on 31 December of the year you cancel in. Until then you are a full member and can use the portal as before.
- **Nothing is refunded.** Dues you have already paid for the year are not paid back.
- **If you are on the waiting list, you lose your place** when the membership ends.
- **A slip or a harbour share (moloandel)** is not settled automatically. The board contacts you about it.
- **Unpaid fakturas** are still owed. The section shows how many you have and links to them.
- **A board role** you hold ends with the membership.

You can write why you are leaving in the optional **Kvifor?** box. Only the board sees it.

After you confirm, you get an email, **Utmelding stadfesta** (Bokmål: **Utmelding bekreftet**). That email is your written confirmation of the cancellation: it states the date your membership ends and that nothing is refunded. Keep it.

### Changing your mind

Until 31 December the section shows **Utmeld frå 31.12.YYYY** with an **Angre** button. Pressing it undoes the cancellation, and you carry on as a member. The board is told. Once your account has been deactivated the cancellation can no longer be undone in the portal; contact the board.

### On 1 January

Your account is deactivated: you can no longer log in, and you are taken off the waiting list. Your fakturas and payment history are kept, as the bookkeeping rules require.

## For the board: following up a cancellation

- **The email.** The board address gets **Utmelding: <name> (til og med 31.12)** when a member cancels, with their reason if they gave one, and a short notice if they undo it. Check whether the member has a slip, a harbour share or a board role, and follow that up.
- **The members list.** A member who has cancelled carries an amber **Utmeld** badge with the end date in **Medlemmer** until the membership ends.
- **Next year's dues.** A bulk faktura run for a period that starts after the member's end date skips them, with the reason "medlemskapen blir avslutta 31.12.YYYY, før perioden startar" in the run's skipped list. The run for the year they cancelled in bills them as usual.
- **1 January.** Brygge deactivates the member and withdraws their waiting-list entries automatically, as the system. If the member is the club's **only administrator**, they are not deactivated: the board gets **Utmelding må handsamast** instead. Make another member an administrator; the next daily run then ends the membership.
- **Taking a member back.** Reactivating a deactivated member under **Medlemmer** also clears their ended cancellation. Their place on the waiting list is **not** restored; enrol them again if they should have one.

How people join in the first place, and how the board opens joining, is described in [join.md](join.md).
